## The questions

### Q1 (SPCX): premium/discount drivers of a tokenized pre-IPO equity claim

> The report shows SPCX trading around 31 with roughly 40M market cap but only about M daily liquidity. If SPCX is a claim on pre-IPO SpaceX equity, what would explain a persistent premium or discount versus implied per-share SpaceX value from secondary markets like Forge or EquityZen: 24/7 on-chain liquidity, redemption friction via Backpack, or speculation on tokenization itself? Which on-chain indicators would help distinguish these drivers?

Why it is a strong question: it does not ask for a price prediction; it asks the model to reconcile conflicting layers of the report (fundamentals vs. market behavior) and to identify which additional indicators would discriminate between competing explanations.

### Q2 (PUMP): fee-multiple regime boundaries

> Pump.fun generates real protocol revenue from launch fees. How should one frame the gap between fee-based fundamental valuation floors and the market meme-cycle pricing? At what annualized fee multiple would the current price imply overheated versus cheap, and which metrics would confirm a regime change?

Why it is a strong question: it forces a quantitative frame (annualized fee multiples) onto a class of asset that is usually analyzed purely qualitatively, and asks for falsifiable regime-change indicators.
